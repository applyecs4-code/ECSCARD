Apply for Your ECS Card in the UK

An ECS card is the recognised ID card for electricians, FESS, data, telecoms and related trades working on electrotechnical sites across the UK.

You can start your ECS card application online using the secure form below.

Different ECS card types exist for labourers, trainees, apprentices, qualified electricians, FESS operatives, network installers, AV technicians, supervisors and managers.
